Ember & Tide — Duel of the Four Elements. A complete, original card battler that plays offline in a desktop browser: 16 unique cards across four elements (Ember, Frost, Tide, Stone), an energy system, a rotating Attunement bonus, Resonance combos for playing two cards of one element, burn and shield mechanics, and a greedy AI opponent — the Sage of the Deep. 30 life each, one table, no mercy.

## Mechanics

- **Energy:** you gain +1 max energy each turn (up to 10) and refill every turn.
- **Attunement:** each turn one element is attuned; its cards deal +2 damage.
- **Resonance:** play two cards of the same element in one turn for +3 damage.
- **Burn & shield:** Ember cards burn, Frost and Stone cards shield, Tide cards heal.
- **The Sage:** a greedy AI that goes for lethal, heals when hurt, and shields when exposed.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a card (or press 1–5) to play it, Space ends your turn.
