EMBER & TIDE

Duel of the Four Elements

A complete card battler. You against the Sage of the Deep — 30 life each, one table, no mercy.

🔥 Ember — burns ❄️ Frost — shields & draws 💧 Tide — heals & crashes 🪨 Stone — hits the hardest
How to play: Each turn you gain energy and draw cards. Play cards to damage the Sage, shield yourself, or heal. Cards matching the turn's Attunement deal +2 damage. Play two cards of the same element in one turn to trigger Resonance (+3 damage). Reduce the Sage to 0 life to win.

Original game · 16 unique cards · No network needed · Keyboard & mouse
